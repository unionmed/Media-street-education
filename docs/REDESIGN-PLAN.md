# Visual redesign plan — 22 September 2026

## Direction

Replace the restrained editorial landing page with a bold learning-and-media studio identity: midnight canvas, luminous lime, off-white reading surfaces and cool violet details. Keep the supplied portraits and bilingual editorial content.

## Composition and interactions

- Immersive split hero with an original code-rendered orbital sculpture, animated paths and a useful three-way pathway selector.
- Oversized editorial headline, clear calls to action, selected-learning strip and a concise content index.
- Prominent team photography, three visually distinct pathway panels, richer programme cards and an image-like graphic journal layout.
- Carry the identity into navigation, footer, catalogue, articles and institutional pages.
- Intersection-based entrance animation, fine-pointer parallax and understated hover choreography. Content stays present without JavaScript. Respect reduced motion and provide an explicit pause control for continuous decorative movement.

## Delivery

Implement responsive English/Arabic components and local CSS/SVG visuals without adding runtime animation dependencies. Verify both export modes, automated accessibility/interaction checks, motion controls, desktop/mobile layouts and live deployment. Commit and deploy through the existing gated Pages workflow.
