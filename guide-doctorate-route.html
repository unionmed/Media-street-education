DOCTORAL STUDY

PhD or professional doctorate: start with the research question

The right doctoral route depends on the problem you want to investigate, the contribution you hope to make and the conditions in which you can sustain the work.

A Merit Way editorial guide · 21 September 2026

In this guide
  1. Look beyond the letters
  2. Describe a question you can investigate
  3. Check access and supervision early
  4. Compare the working conditions

Look beyond the letters

A PhD commonly centres on an original research contribution. Professional doctorates such as a DBA often connect doctoral research with a field of professional practice. These are orientations, not a universal rule about every programme. Portsmouth’s DBA, for example, is framed around researching a business problem and influencing practice. Compare the actual research expectations, taught components and assessment regulations of each programme.

Describe a question you can investigate

Replace “I want to study leadership” with a bounded question about a setting, group or process. What puzzles you? What evidence could help? Why would answering it matter beyond your own experience? You do not need a finished thesis idea to begin exploring, but you do need enough specificity to identify a department and a possible supervisory fit.

Check access and supervision early

For a workplace-based question, ask whether the information and participants would remain accessible if your job changed. Consider confidentiality, permissions and research ethics before promising a dataset. Read prospective supervisors’ recent work and compare their methods with your interests. An attractive university name cannot replace a credible match between the project, expertise and available supervision.

Compare the working conditions

Build a comparison covering attendance, milestones, supervision frequency, progression reviews, full fees and your intended use of the qualification. Ask the university to clarify ambiguous points in writing. Recognition and employment expectations depend on the destination and role, so check those directly. A strong next step is a short concept note and a focused academic enquiry, not choosing solely by the title.
