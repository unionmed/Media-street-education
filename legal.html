Affiliate and referral disclosure

Merit Way, operated by Media Street S.A.R.L., may in the future receive a commission when a visitor uses a clearly identified affiliate or referral link and completes an eligible purchase or registration with the provider. This does not add a fee to the learner unless the provider explicitly states otherwise.

At launch, programme links on this site are direct provider links and are not represented as affiliate links. If that changes, we will identify the relationship near the relevant link.

Editorial approach

We aim to describe programmes accurately, explain their intended learner and link to the provider’s current information. We do not guarantee employment, promotion, admission, recognition, licensing, completion or a particular learning outcome. Programme availability, fees and terms are determined by the provider and may change.

Education and admissions

Merit Way does not award degrees or certificates, make admission decisions, collect tuition for listed providers, or represent itself as an accredited educational agent unless a specific formal agreement says so. Any future recruitment or referral relationship will be described accurately.

Privacy

This static website does not use a contact form, user accounts or advertising tracking cookies. If you choose to email us, we use the information you provide only to respond to your enquiry and for related communication. Do not send sensitive identity, financial or academic documents by email unless we ask for them through an appropriate agreed process.
