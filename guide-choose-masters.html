HIGHER EDUCATION

Choosing a master’s that fits your next chapter

A useful shortlist begins with the work you want to do and the life you need to study around. Build the comparison before falling in love with a university name.

A Merit Way editorial guide · 21 September 2026

In this guide
  1. Write the decision in one sentence
  2. Compare modules and assessment
  3. Test the timetable against a real week
  4. Make a decision sheet, not a ranking

Write the decision in one sentence

Complete this sentence: “I want this degree to help me move from … to …”. A change of profession, deeper specialist practice and preparation for research are different objectives. Pick the primary one. Then write down what would make the degree a poor fit: too much repetition, a timetable you cannot attend or too little research preparation. This makes your shortlist easier to defend.

Compare modules and assessment

Choose three programmes and list their required modules, optional choices, final project and assessment methods. Mark the parts that support your objective. Ask how much choice is actually available in your intake. A promising module title is less useful than knowing what you will read, produce and receive feedback on. For a future research route, investigate the research training and dissertation arrangements in particular.

Test the timetable against a real week

Put study sessions in your calendar alongside work, care responsibilities and travel. Leave room for assessment peaks. The Open University highlights the greater independence and intensity involved in postgraduate work; use that as a prompt to ask each provider for realistic weekly expectations. For distance study, check live attendance, time zones, examinations and any required visits instead of assuming that online means fully asynchronous.

Make a decision sheet, not a ranking

Use five headings: academic fit, delivery, support, full cost and the requirements of your intended employer or next institution. Record evidence and unanswered questions beside each. Verify recognition with the relevant destination authority when it matters to your goal. Your next action is a precise enquiry to the university, with the three questions that could change your choice.
